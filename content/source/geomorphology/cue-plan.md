# Geomorphology board videos: cue plan

Each video is made of slides shown for **7 seconds each**, so cue *n* starts at **(n − 1) × 7 s**.
"Point" is where the teacher points (fraction of width, height from the top-left), as used by the cue editor.
Narration is the English draft; Telugu and Hindi are drafted by the lesson creator and need native review.

## Video 1: Forces that shape the land (`geo-v1-forces.mp4`, 28 s)
| # | At | Slide | Point | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0 s | What is geomorphology? | 0.23, 0.30 | Geomorphology is the study of landforms, like mountains, valleys, plains and deltas, and of how they are made and changed. |
| 2 | 7 s | Endogenic forces | 0.50, 0.26 | Endogenic forces come from inside the Earth. Its heat pushes land up, making volcanoes, earthquakes and rising mountains. |
| 3 | 14 s | Exogenic forces | 0.67, 0.35 | Exogenic forces work on the surface. The sun, rain, rivers, wind, ice and waves slowly wear the land down. |
| 4 | 21 s | Tug-of-war | 0.50, 0.45 | Every landform is a tug-of-war: forces inside push land up, and forces outside wear it down. |

## Check questions (between videos)
1. After video 1: *Is a volcano made by a force from inside the Earth?* (yes)
2. After video 2: *When a river drops sand at the sea, is that called deposition?* (yes)
3. After video 3: *Does a glacier make a U-shaped valley?* (yes)
4. After video 4: *Is a river with waterfalls and a deep V-valley in its youth stage?* (yes)
